Emergency Medical Alert

If you are experiencing severe liver-related complications, please seek immediate emergency care:

  • Severe or acute confusion / disorientation (Hepatic Encephalopathy)
  • Sudden severe abdominal pain or swelling
  • Vomiting blood or passing dark, tarry stools
  • Rapid worsening of jaundice (yellowing of eyes/skin)

phoneEmergency Hotline: 911

Please notify your licensed medical practitioner immediately regarding these symptoms.
